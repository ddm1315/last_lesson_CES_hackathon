The central question progresses from **“What is happening?”** to **“How did useful AI become a substitute for human thinking?”**

## Narrative rules

- C remains mysterious until Stage 10. Early evidence may suggest a system failure, but it must not state the final conclusion.
- Evidence entries describe only what the participant has established at that stage. They should not claim that six institutions, an agent, or the full cause has been proven unless the activity actually establishes it.
- The participant is always in the present. C’s dates explain why the records appear out of order.
- The app validates participation and answer shape, not whether an external ChatGPT activity was genuinely completed.
- “Institution” is the canonical term for BYU, BYU–Idaho, BYU–Hawaii, Ensign College, BYU-Pathway, and Church. “Campus” may be used when referring to a physical or organizational location.

## Debrief handoff

Stage 10 should clearly tell the participant to copy or otherwise preserve their email before leaving the page. The response is intentionally not stored by the app, but the debrief—not the application—is where the recommendation becomes useful.
